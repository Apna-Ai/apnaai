Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about using ApnaAI.

How do I use ApnaAI?

Open the home page, choose a tool, enter your request and select Generate with AI.

Which languages are supported?

ApnaAI is designed to respond in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu. The response language generally follows your request.

Do I need an account?

At present, the AI tools can be used without signing in. Google Login is not yet enabled.

Why might an AI request be slow or fail?

Requests depend on the availability and usage limits of the external AI provider. If a request fails, try again later.

Can AI answers contain mistakes?

Yes. Check facts, translations and other important content before using it.

Is ApnaAI free?

Access and availability may change. Any applicable limits will depend on the AI service configuration.
