Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about using ApnaAI and understanding its limits.

How do I use an AI tool?

Open the ApnaAI home page, choose a tool card, enter the details of your task and select Generate with AI. Include the intended audience, tone, length and any facts the answer must preserve. Review the result before copying or publishing it.

What does each tool do?

AI Text Writer helps draft and organize text; Caption Generator suggests social captions; CV Maker structures career information; Email Writer drafts messages; Product Description turns supplied product facts into sales copy; Translator creates a first translation; Study Assistant explains topics and prepares practice questions; Bio Generator drafts short profile descriptions.

Which languages can I use?

ApnaAI is designed for English, Urdu and Roman Urdu prompts. You can also specify the language you want in the answer. Results may vary, so ask a fluent speaker to check important translations.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is currently required to use the AI tools. Google Login is not enabled at this time.

Is my prompt sent to another company?

Yes. The text you submit is sent to Google Gemini so it can generate a response. Cloudflare provides website hosting. Do not enter passwords, payment details, private identification numbers or confidential information. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can AI answers contain mistakes?

Yes. AI can misunderstand a request, omit important details or produce inaccurate information. Verify names, dates, figures, product specifications and translations. Do not rely on an AI response as professional medical, legal, financial or safety advice.

Why is a response slow or unsuccessful?

Generation depends on the external AI provider, internet connectivity and available usage quota. If you see a temporary service error, wait briefly and try again. If the daily provider quota has been reached, requests may not work again until the quota resets or the service owner changes the API plan.

What should I include in a good prompt?

State the task, audience, tone, format and length. Add only accurate details. For example, instead of “write a CV,” ask for “a one-page CV for an entry-level office assistant role using only the education, skills and work history I provide.”

See the practical prompt guide and our article on using AI writing tools wisely for more examples.

Can I publish AI-generated content directly?

You are responsible for checking and editing anything you publish. Confirm factual claims, remove unsupported statements, make the wording useful to your audience and ensure you have rights to any material you provide. AI output should not be treated as automatically original, accurate or ready to publish.

Is ApnaAI always free?

There is currently no sign-in or payment flow on the website. AI generation still depends on provider availability and usage limits, which can change. We do not promise uninterrupted or unlimited generation.

How can I report a problem?

Email ranagrub6@gmail.com with the page or tool name and a short description. Do not send passwords, API keys or other secret information.
