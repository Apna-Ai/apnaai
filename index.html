How to get a useful result from ApnaAI

A clear request usually produces a more useful first draft. Tell the tool what you are creating, who it is for, the tone you want and any important details it must include.

1. Choose the right tool

Select a writer, caption, CV, email, translator, study or bio tool that matches your task.

2. Give useful context

Include your audience, purpose, language, length and facts the draft should use. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal data.

3. Review before sharing

Check names, numbers, facts, tone and translations. AI output is a draft and can contain errors.
