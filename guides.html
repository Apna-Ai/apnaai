Practical Guide to Getting Better Results with ApnaAI

A step-by-step guide to writing clearer prompts, choosing the right tool and checking the result before you use it.

ApnaAI provides a set of writing and learning tools. The quality of an AI draft depends partly on the instructions you provide. A short request can be useful, but a request with context, audience and a clear goal usually gives the AI a better starting point.

1. Use a simple prompt structure

When you write a request, try to include four things: the task, the audience, the tone and the format. Add facts that must be included, and say what should be avoided.

Basic request: Write a product description.
More useful request: Write a friendly 120-word product description for a reusable stainless-steel water bottle for university students. Mention its capacity, leak-resistant lid and easy cleaning. Do not invent a warranty or claim it keeps drinks cold for a specific number of hours.

The second request gives the AI a defined audience, length, features and an important accuracy boundary.

2. Examples for each ApnaAI tool

AI Text Writer

Use this tool when you need a first draft, outline or rewrite. Tell it the topic, intended reader, length and structure.

Create a 500-word beginner-friendly article explaining how to prepare for a job interview. Use clear headings, practical steps and a supportive tone. Avoid made-up statistics.

Caption Generator

State the platform, subject, audience and mood. If you want hashtags, ask for a small relevant set rather than a long unrelated list.

Write three short Instagram captions for a local bakery introducing its fresh almond cookies. Keep the tone warm and natural. Add up to five relevant hashtags. Do not claim the cookies are sugar-free.

CV Maker

Provide accurate education, experience, skills and the role you are applying for. Do not ask AI to invent qualifications, employers or achievements.

Turn these real details into a concise CV summary for an entry-level customer support role. Emphasize communication, problem-solving and computer skills. Do not add experience I have not listed.

Email Writer

Explain who will receive the email, why you are writing and what response or action you need.

Draft a polite email to a course instructor asking whether I can submit an assignment one day late. Briefly explain that I had a family commitment, take responsibility and ask if an extension is possible.

Product Description

Share verified product specifications and the intended customer. Avoid unsupported health, performance, safety or comparison claims.

Write a clear online-store description using only these details: cotton fabric, navy colour, sizes S to XL and machine-washable. Mention the fit as regular. Do not invent customer reviews.

Translator

Specify the source and target language, desired level of formality and whether names or technical terms should remain unchanged. Review important translations with a fluent speaker.

Translate this customer-service message from English into natural Pakistani Urdu. Keep the tone respectful and conversational. Preserve the order number exactly as written.

Study Assistant

Tell it your level and ask for an explanation in stages. Ask for examples or practice questions when they help you learn.

Explain photosynthesis to a class 8 student in simple English. Use one everyday analogy, list the main stages and finish with five short revision questions and their answers.

Bio Generator

Include the platform, your real interests or work and the tone you want. Avoid adding credentials or personal details that are not true.

Write three short professional profile bios for a beginner graphic designer. Use only these facts: I am learning brand design, I enjoy clean layouts and I am building a portfolio.

3. Write in the language you are comfortable with

You can write a request in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. Mention the language you want in the final result if it is different from the language of your prompt. For example: “Explain this in Roman Urdu with simple words” or “Isay asaan Urdu mein samjha dein.”

4. Check AI output before using it

  • Check names, dates, prices, measurements and other specific facts.
  • Compare claims against reliable sources, especially for health, law, money, education and safety.
  • Make sure a CV or application describes your real experience and skills.
  • Read translations for tone and meaning, not just spelling.
  • Edit the draft so it reflects your own knowledge, voice and purpose.

5. Protect your privacy

Do not enter passwords, payment card details, private identification numbers or confidential business information into prompts. Text submitted to an AI tool is sent to the AI provider to generate a response. Read the ApnaAI Privacy Policy before using the service.

6. Understand service limits

AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect. The service also depends on the external AI provider and its availability and usage limits. If generation fails because a provider limit has been reached, try again after the limit resets.
