Privacy Policy

How ApnaAI handles information when you use this website.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Information you enter

When you submit a prompt to an AI tool, the text you provide is sent to our AI service provider, Google Gemini, so it can generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or anything you do not want processed by an AI provider.

Registration form

If you submit the registration form, the name, email address and optional phone number you provide are sent to the ApnaAI contact email through FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service. Do not enter a password or sensitive personal information. This form sends a registration enquiry only; it does not create a secure user account or provide login access. FormSubmit may process submitted information under its own privacy policy and service terms.

Google sign-in and password-based accounts are not currently enabled.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as request and security logs, to deliver and protect the website.

Third-party services

AI requests are processed by Google Gemini and website hosting is provided by Cloudflare. Their services are subject to their own privacy terms and policies.

Cookies and advertising

ApnaAI does not currently display Google AdSense ads. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and help personalize ads based on visits to this website or other websites. Third-party vendors may place and read cookies in your browser as part of ad delivery.

You can learn how Google uses information from partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about opting out of some third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

If advertising is introduced, we will update this policy to identify the advertising services actually in use and provide any required consent choices. Where applicable, consent will be requested before relevant advertising technologies are used.

Data retention

ApnaAI does not currently provide a user account or prompt history feature. Processing and retention by third-party providers are governed by their policies and service settings.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
