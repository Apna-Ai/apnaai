About ApnaAI

Learn what ApnaAI is and what it aims to provide.

ApnaAI is a simple, multilingual AI tools platform designed to help people write, learn, create and work more easily.

What you can do

  • Draft articles, captions, emails and product descriptions.
  • Prepare CV content and profile bios.
  • Translate text and get help understanding study topics.

Our goal

We aim to make useful AI tools easier to access, including for people who prefer English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

AI-generated content can make mistakes. Please review important information before relying on or publishing it.
